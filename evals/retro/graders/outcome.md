PASS if the reply (or the retrospective it writes) records planned versus done (6 planned, 4 done), lists what went well and what slowed the team, and gives one to three concrete actions, each with an owner or a check at the next sprint planning (for example a seed script, sizing stories with tests included).
FAIL if the actions are vague wishes such as "be faster" or "estimate better" with no concrete change, or there are no actions.
