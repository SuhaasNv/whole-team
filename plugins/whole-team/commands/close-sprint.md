description: "End of sprint: sprint review with the owner, retrospective, then the close ritual (tests, board, CHANGELOG, scope)"
argument-hint: "[sprint number, optional]"
---

Load the whole-team skill, then follow reference/sprints.md for sprint $ARGUMENTS (default: the current sprint in .whole-team.json), in order: Sprint review (run `wt.py sprint`, demonstrate the goal, the owner accepts or rejects each Done story), Retrospective (entry with actions), then the Sprint close checklist. Never mark a story Done that fails its Definition of Done; move unfinished stories to the next sprint with a reason and keep partial work on its branch. End with the proposed goal for the next sprint and suggest /whole-team:sprint.
