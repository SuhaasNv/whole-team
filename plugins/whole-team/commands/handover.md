description: "Before you stop: record what is half-done and what to run next, so the next session continues without re-explaining"
---

Load the whole-team skill. For every story In progress: check its branch, what is committed, what is uncommitted, and what is left against its acceptance criteria. Commit finished work in progress on the story branch (never merge partial work). Add one `Handover (<date>): ...` line per story at the top of CHANGELOG.md with what is done, what is left and the next command to run, and mark handover lines that are now resolved with (resolved). Update the board if anything drifted. End with a three-line summary for the owner.
