Load the whole-team skill (it holds the rules, the reference files and the wt.py script), then follow the Retrospective section of reference/sprints.md for sprint $ARGUMENTS (default: the current sprint). Start from `wt.py sprint` (planned vs done) and the sprint's history (git log, CHANGELOG, slipped stories, change requests). Check last sprint's actions first. Write the entry in docs/05-planning/RETROSPECTIVES.md (lite: three lines in CHANGELOG.md) with one to three concrete actions, each with an owner and "check at next sprint planning". Write the full entry from what you know first, then ask the owner what to add; ask about missing facts only after showing the draft.
