Use the whole-team skill and follow the Retrospective section of reference/sprints.md for sprint $ARGUMENTS (default: the current sprint). Start from `wt.py sprint` (planned vs done) and the sprint's history (git log, CHANGELOG, slipped stories, change requests). Check last sprint's actions first. Write the entry in docs/05-planning/RETROSPECTIVES.md (lite: three lines in CHANGELOG.md) with one to three concrete actions, each with an owner and "check at next sprint planning". Ask the owner what they would add before saving.
