---
description: "Deliver the next story (or a named one) through the full story loop"
argument-hint: "[US-id]"
---

Load the whole-team skill (it holds the rules, the reference files and the wt.py script), then follow reference/story-loop.md for story $ARGUMENTS (if empty, take "Next up" from `wt.py status`). Pull, start (board In progress, branch), plan, build, verify, hats pass, independent review, Definition of Done, local --no-ff merge, board Done, one-line status. Do not push without the owner's yes in this turn.
